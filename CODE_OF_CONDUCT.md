# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate, regardless of background, identity, ability,
or experience. Be considerate, give specific technical feedback, and accept
corrections without personal attacks. Critique the work, not the person.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized abuse, and sharing someone's
private information without consent are not acceptable. These expectations
apply to issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close a discussion, warn a participant,
or restrict participation for repeated or serious misconduct. Decisions should
protect participants and explain what needs to change.

Use GitHub's **Report content** action to report abuse privately to GitHub.
[GitHub's reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
explains that process. Do not post private incident details in a public issue.

These expectations draw on the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
